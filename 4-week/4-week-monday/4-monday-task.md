# Monday 4 – Clustering Investigation

Use your **Week 3 Clustering Notebook** and `synthetic-clustering-dataset.csv`.

Choose **one clustering result that you want to understand better**.

Possible questions:

* Why did scaling change the clusters?
* Why is one cluster very small?
* Why does changing `k` change the result?
* Why is the silhouette score higher for one solution?
* What happens if one feature is removed?
* Why do k-means and hierarchical clustering disagree?
* Is one feature dominating the clustering?
* Are the clusters actually meaningful?

## Investigate

Change or compare **one thing** and rerun the analysis.

Record briefly:

**1. Question**
What are you trying to understand?

**2. Experiment**
What did you change or compare?

**3. Evidence**
What happened?

**4. Interpretation**
What do you think explains the result?

## Discuss with your group

Compare your investigations.

Choose one finding or unresolved question that is worth discussing with others.

**The goal is not to find the “best” clustering. The goal is to understand why the clustering behaves as it does.**
