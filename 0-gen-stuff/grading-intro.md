The course follows a **flipped classroom approach**. Students study introductory material before each Monday session, and classroom time is used mainly for explanations, demonstrations, guided exercises, problem solving, and project work.

A typical week consists of:

* preparation material and a short automatically graded quiz before Monday;
* a 5-hour Monday teaching and workshop session;
* independent practical exercises during the week;
* project work and preparation for the next topic.

Assessment is based on several complementary components:

* **Preparation quizzes and concept maps – 10%**
* **Practical exercises and automated verification – 20%**
* **Practical data processing and machine learning group project – 30%**
* **Individual demonstration of project understanding – 10%**
* **Final individual exam – 30%**

The final exam uses mainly automatically graded questions and assesses understanding of the main concepts, methods, and their appropriate use. The group project assesses the ability to apply data handling and machine learning methods to a practical problem, including data preparation, method selection, validation, visualization, and interpretation.

Both **individual understanding and practical application** are important. Students are therefore expected not only to use machine learning tools, but also to understand why particular methods and preprocessing choices are appropriate for the data and problem being studied.
