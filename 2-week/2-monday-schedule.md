## 13:30–14:30 Project Data Investigation

Work with your project group.

Start from the project problem, not from a machine-learning method.

Determine:

* What are we trying to find out?
* What could one observation represent?
* Which data sources may be useful?
* Which variables may be relevant?
* How can the datasets be connected?
* What data-quality problems may occur?

## 14:45–15:35 Project Data Plan

Prepare a short **Project Data Plan** containing:

1. **Problem** – What are you trying to find out?
2. **Unit of analysis** – What does one observation represent?
3. **Data sources** – Which data may be needed?
4. **Variables** – Which variables may be useful?
5. **Relationships** – How will datasets be connected?
6. **Data problems** – What quality, missingness, interpretation or privacy issues may occur?
7. **Next step** – What should be prepared or investigated first?

No machine-learning model is required yet.
