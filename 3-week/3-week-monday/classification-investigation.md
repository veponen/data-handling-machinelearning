# Monday 3 – Classification Investigation

Work first individually, then discuss with your group.

## Task

Choose **one classification result from your Week 2 notebook that you do not fully understand**.

Examples:

* Why is the baseline surprisingly strong?
* Why is recall low?
* Why do two classifiers give different results?
* Why does the result change with another train/test split?
* What happens if one feature is removed?
* Is class imbalance affecting the result?
* Could preprocessing cause data leakage?
* Is one feature giving the model information it should not have?

## Investigate

Use:

* your notebook;
* course material;
* the assigned sources;
* scikit-learn documentation;
* discussion with your group.

You may modify and rerun your notebook.

Record briefly:

**1. Question**
What are you trying to understand?

**2. Investigation**
What did you change, compare or check?

**3. Evidence**
What happened?

**4. Conclusion**
What do you now think explains the result?

## Group discussion

Compare your investigations.

Choose **one finding, problem or unanswered question** your group considers worth discussing with the class.

## Important

The goal is **not to maximize model accuracy**.

Try to understand:

> what the model is doing, what mistakes it makes, and whether the result can be trusted.
