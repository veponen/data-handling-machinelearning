# Week 1 – Independent Study Timetable

After the first Monday, plan approximately **28 hours of independent study** before the next contact session.

The schedule below is a suggested way to keep the work within normal weekday hours. You may adjust the timing, but complete the required work before Monday.

| Day           | Suggested time | Main work                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | -------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tuesday**   |       **~7 h** | **Study the Week 2 theory** – data types and meaning, data acquisition, data quality, cleaning and preprocessing. Complete the small introductory exercises included with the material. Resolve any remaining essential tool problems. |
| **Wednesday** |       **~7 h** | **Data understanding exercise** – explore the provided dataset, identify variable types and meanings, find quality problems, and consider suitable operations and possible analysis questions.                                         |
| **Thursday**  |       **~7 h** | **Data preparation and management** – continue practical work with cleaning, transformation and basic data-management concepts. Prepare your first **simplified concept map** based on the theory studied during the week.             |
| **Friday**    |       **~7 h** | **Complete and review** – finish practical exercises, complete the preparation quiz/task, review difficult concepts, and make sure your concept map and other required work are ready for Monday.                                      |

## Before next Monday

You should have:

* studied the assigned Week 2 theory;
* completed the required introductory and practical exercises;
* prepared your first **simplified concept map**;
* completed the preparation quiz/task;
* resolved essential problems with the required course tools.

The goal is to arrive on Monday already familiar with the basic concepts. Monday can then be used for **clarification, deeper exercises, problem solving, discussion and guided practical work**.
