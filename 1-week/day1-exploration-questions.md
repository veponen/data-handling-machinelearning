# Day 1 – Dataset Exploration

Work with your group. Do not start cleaning the data yet. First try to understand what the dataset represents and whether it can be trusted.

1. **What does one row represent?** Is that completely clear from the dataset alone?

2. **What does each variable mean?** Identify the identifier(s), categorical variables, ordinal variables, numerical variables, and datetime variables. For numerical variables, consider whether they are discrete or continuous.

3. **What data-quality problems can you find?** Look for missing values, inconsistent representations, impossible or suspicious values, duplicates, and technical data-type problems.

4. **Which summaries or calculations would be meaningful for each variable?** Give at least one example of a calculation that would *not* make sense even if the computer allows it.

5. **What questions could this dataset reasonably help answer?** Give two examples. Also give one question that this dataset cannot answer reliably.

6. **What additional information would you want before trusting or interpreting the data?** Consider definitions, units, time period, how values were collected, and what missing values mean.

7. **If you had to prepare this dataset for later analysis, what would you do first?** Choose two issues and explain the decisions you would need to make before changing the data.
