# First Monday – Student Schedule

**Data Processing and Machine Learning**

The first Monday starts the course and prepares you for the weekly working rhythm. You will form your group, check that the required tools work, explore your first dataset, and practise making a concept map.

| Time            | What we will do                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **09:00–09:25** | **Course introduction** – course goals, workload, assessment and how the weekly studies work                         |
| **09:25–09:40** | **Group formation** – find your assigned group and sit together                                                      |
| **09:40–11:10** | **Technical readiness** – check GitHub, Git, VS Code, Python, Jupyter and required tools together with your group    |
| **11:10–11:20** | Break                                                                                                                |
| **11:20–12:00** | **Data processing, analytics and machine learning** – what they are, how they differ and where they are used         |
| **12:00–13:00** | Lunch                                                                                                                |
| **13:00–13:40** | **Explore a dataset** – investigate variables, possible problems and questions that could be answered using the data |
| **13:40–14:15** | **Understand the data** – data types, meaning of variables and what kinds of operations make sense                   |
| **14:15–14:30** | Break                                                                                                                |
| **14:30–15:10** | **Concept maps** – how to select concepts and show meaningful relationships between them                             |
| **15:10–15:35** | **Group concept-map exercise** – create and edit a shared concept map in draw.io                                     |
| **15:35–16:00** | **Start Week 1 independent study** – materials, exercises, concept map and preparation for next Monday               |

### By the end of the day

You should:

* know how the course and weekly studies work;
* know your initial group;
* have a working environment for the course exercises;
* understand the basic data-to-machine-learning workflow;
* have practised exploring and interpreting data;
* know how to prepare the weekly concept map;
* know exactly what to complete before the next Monday.
